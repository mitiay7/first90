Privacy by design

Support without surveillance.

First 90 separates personal reflection from team-level signals. This public demo uses fictional sample data.

Private by default

Journal entries and AI coach conversations belong to the participant. Team Studio never exposes their text.

Minimal signals

Team views use progress, activity, and consented mood scores to identify where a human check-in may help. They do not diagnose performance or wellbeing.

AI boundaries

The coach receives role context, program day, transition context, work mode, and the question. It does not receive private journal history. A privacy-preserving safety identifier is sent with live OpenAI requests.

Demo data

All names, emails, reflections, and metrics in the public demo are fictional. The demo can be reset at any time.
