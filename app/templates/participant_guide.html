01
DAILY RHYTHM

Focus, action, reflection

Each day has three short touchpoints. Focus names the useful lens. Action turns it into one observable move. Reflection captures what changed. Complete them in order; progress is saved automatically.

◎ FocusAbout 4 minutes↗ ActionAbout 8 minutes⌁ ReflectionAbout 5 minutes
02
OPTIONAL SIGNAL

Record energy, not performance

Choose 1–5 to mark your current energy. There is no “good employee” score. The pattern helps you notice change and helps the team offer support without reading private content.

03
PRIVATE SPACE

Write one honest sentence

Use the journal when naming a fact, surprise, or next adjustment would help. Team Studio never receives journal text. In the public demo, Reset demo restores fictional starting data.

04
GPT‑5.6 COACH

Pressure-test the next move

Ask about a conversation, assumption, decision, or conflict. The coach returns a practical read, a move for the next 24 hours, and something to notice. It is workplace guidance, not therapy, legal advice, or an automated decision-maker.

TRY ASKING

“Two sponsors gave me conflicting priorities. What should I clarify first?”

05
OPTIONAL CHANNEL

Start your adaptation in Telegram

Open the bot link supplied by your program admin, tap Start, or send /start. The bot creates your journey through six short questions.

  1. Step 1 of 6: enter the name the bot should use.
  2. Step 2 of 6: select People Manager, the role supported in early access.
  3. Step 3 of 6: choose same or new company.
  4. Step 4 of 6: choose same or new city.
  5. Step 5 of 6: select a supported European city, such as Riga, Berlin, or Paris. This sets the delivery time zone.
  6. Step 6 of 6: choose office, hybrid, or remote work.

Your tailored Day 1 focus arrives immediately. Use /today for the current touchpoint, /mood 1-5 for energy, /pause to pause or resume, and /web for the optional web demo. Plain text in the private bot chat becomes a private journal entry. Send /cancel to leave an unfinished setup.
