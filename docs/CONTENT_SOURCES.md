# Enhanced Days 1–3 content

The enhanced path targets a People Manager promoted within the same company while staying in the same city. It avoids generic newcomer advice and focuses on identity, listening, changed peer relationships, and the real trust network.

## Selected learning resources

Day 1:

- [How to be an effective boss: the identity shift](https://www.ccl.org/articles/leading-effectively-articles/identity-shift-achieving-results-by-managing-others/)
- [The 12 common challenges of new managers](https://www.ccl.org/articles/leading-effectively-articles/first-time-managers-must-conquer-these-challenges/)
- [First-time managers: it is not about me anymore](https://www.ccl.org/wp-content/uploads/2015/05/not-about-me-first-time-managers-research-paper-center-for-creative-leadership.pdf)

Day 2:

- [Listen to understand and ask powerful questions](https://www.ccl.org/articles/leading-effectively-articles/how-to-use-coaching-and-mentoring-programs-to-develop-new-leaders/)
- [Google re:Work guide to team effectiveness](https://rework.withgoogle.com/en/guides/understanding-team-effectiveness)
- [Is it safe to speak up at work?](https://www.ted.com/talks/worklife_with_adam_grant_is_it_safe_to_speak_up_at_work)

Day 3:

- [Clarify team roles and responsibilities](https://www.atlassian.com/team-playbook/plays/roles-and-responsibilities)
- [Network of Teams play](https://www.atlassian.com/team-playbook/plays/network-of-teams)
- [Create working agreements with your team](https://www.atlassian.com/team-playbook/plays/working-agreements)

Links point to the original publishers. First 90 summarizes no source article and republishes no third-party illustration.

## Original illustrations

The three WebP assets under `app/static/illustrations/` were generated specifically for First 90 with the built-in OpenAI image-generation workflow. Shared art direction: contemporary editorial workplace illustration, subtle paper grain, warm ivory, deep navy, muted coral, sage, diverse adult teams, European workplace cues, no text, no logos, and no watermark.

- Day 1 prompt: an internally promoted manager enters a familiar office and sees the same team through a new responsibility.
- Day 2 prompt: a newly promoted manager listens to a former peer in a psychologically safe one-to-one.
- Day 3 prompt: a familiar team collaboratively maps how work and trust flow across relationships.
