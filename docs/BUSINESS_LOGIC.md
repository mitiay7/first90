# Business logic

## Journey

- Length: 90 days.
- Daily touchpoints: focus, action, reflection.
- Universal role coverage: 270 published capsules.
- Enhanced context coverage: nine additional Days 1–3 capsules for a same-company, same-city People Manager.
- Phases:
  - Days 1–30: Listen & orient.
  - Days 31–60: Align & deliver.
  - Days 61–90: Scale & sustain.

## Transition context

Guidance can adapt to:

- role;
- same or new company;
- same or new city;
- office, hybrid, or remote work mode;
- current program day and touchpoint.

The city reference list uses European examples and IANA timezones. `city` and `city_context` are intentionally separate: one schedules correctly, the other explains the transition.

## Signals

Mood is a voluntary 1–5 energy signal, not a diagnosis or performance score. The participant sees their own pattern. Team Studio sees only aggregate and risk-label outputs.

## Control

Participants can pause or resume. Progress advances only after the active touchpoint is completed. A completion is idempotent.

Telegram enrollment asks for name, role, company context, city context, European city, and work mode. A participant record is created only after all six answers are valid. Early access currently accepts People Manager as the content-ready role.

## Access phases

- Phase 1, current: free early access with no payment gateway or billing data.
- Phase 2, planned: hosted checkout, paid plans, entitlements, idempotent payment webhooks, cancellation, and refund handling.
